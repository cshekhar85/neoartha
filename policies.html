Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you buy a report from NeoArtha, we collect the details needed to complete and deliver the purchase: your name, email address, and the transaction details generated by our payment partner. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details directly — these are handled by our payment processor (Razorpay) and our storefront platform (Superprofile), each under their own security and privacy terms.

How we use it

  • To deliver the report you purchased, by email or through a download link
  • To respond to support, refund, or access requests you send us
  • To send you occasional updates about new reports, if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third parties

Purchases are processed through Superprofile and Razorpay. Each of these providers processes limited data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies. We share with them only what's needed to complete your order and deliver your report.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by writing to contact@neoartha.in. We'll respond within a reasonable time and confirm once it's done.

Cookies

Our site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited. This does not identify you personally and is used only to improve the site.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Reports are digital and delivered instantly, which shapes how refunds work.

Because each report is delivered digitally and instantly on successful payment, all sales are final. We do not offer refunds once a report has been delivered to you.

The one exception is a duplicate or erroneous charge — if you were charged more than once for the same order, or charged in error, we will refund the extra amount in full.

If payment was successful but you did not receive your report, that isn't treated as a refund case — write to us and we'll resend it or fix the delivery issue directly, at no extra cost to you.

To raise either of these, email contact@neoartha.in with your order/payment reference within 7 days of purchase. We'll review and respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, subject to your bank or payment provider's own timelines.

As there's no physical product, there's nothing to cancel once a report has been delivered.

Delivery Policy

NeoArtha sells digital reports only — nothing is physically shipped.

All NeoArtha reports are digital PDFs. There is no physical shipping involved. On successful payment, your report is delivered either as an instant download link or by email, typically within a few minutes. If you haven't received your report within 24 hours of a successful payment, please write to contact@neoartha.in with your order reference and we'll resend it or resolve the issue.
